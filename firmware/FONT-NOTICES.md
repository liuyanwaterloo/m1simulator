# Font sources and licensing

The M1 menu atlas is a composite resource. Do not assume one license covers everything.

- Existing ASCII (U+0020–U+007E) is preserved byte-for-byte from the supplied `Font/Helvetica16.c`: Helvetica Regular, generated by SEGGER Font Converter **Demo V6.40**, copyright (c) SEGGER Microcontroller GmbH 1998–2024. The project also contains demo-marked icons. Ownership/commercial redistribution rights are NOT established by this work; confirm before commercial shipment. Original source SHA256 is in the manifest.
- New Chinese subset derives from **Noto Sans CJK SC Regular**, official source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Sans/OTF/SimplifiedChinese . License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; included as `assets/fonts/OFL-CJK.txt`.
- New Cyrillic subset derives from **Noto Sans Regular**, official source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/tree/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans . License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; included as `assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSans.txt`.

Original font binaries are included outside the deployed public directory for repeatable generation, together with license notices and their embedded copyright metadata. Font SHA256 values, rasterizer version, pixel format and counts are recorded in `shared/font-manifest.json`. Noto-derived bitmap portions remain under OFL 1.1; the composite output is called **M1 Menu**, not an official Noto release.
